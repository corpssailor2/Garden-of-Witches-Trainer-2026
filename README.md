## 🎮 About the Game

Garden of Witches is a fast-paced action roguelike developed by Team Tapas. It entered Early Access on May 23, 2025, and reached full release on August 28, 2026. The game combines action, RPG, mod-and-slash, and dungeon-crawling elements in a hand-drawn 2D isometric world.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Garden of Witches**. This trainer provides optional gameplay enhancements for Garden of Witches, including improved survivability, faster ability recovery, stronger attacks, and greater control over movement and run progression. It is designed around the game's scissors-based combat, spells, special skills, relics, imprints, traits, and randomized roguelike encounters.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Garden of Witches more freely, practice boss attack patterns, test scissors and spell combinations, experiment with relic and imprint synergies, or reduce the difficulty of repeated roguelike runs.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents Sil from losing health during exploration and combat.
- **Infinite Health** — Maintains the current health value during a run without preventing other gameplay effects.
- **Run Resource Control** — Provides control over available run-based resources used to shape builds through rewards, upgrades, and other progression choices.
- **Freeze Game** — Pauses gameplay logic while leaving the trainer interface available.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Instant Cooldowns** — Refreshes cooldown-based spells, special skills, and related abilities immediately after use.
- **Unlimited Charges** — Keeps charge-based abilities available for repeated use, including charged scissors attacks and other skills that rely on stored charges.
- **Damage Multiplier** — Applies a selectable damage multiplier to weapon attacks, spells, and special skills.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Allows standard enemies and bosses to be defeated with greatly increased damage.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Unlimited Dash** — Removes the normal dash cooldown to support rapid repositioning during enemy attack patterns.
- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts Sil's movement speed for faster traversal and more responsive positioning.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Garden of Witches uses isometric movement with keyboard controls and mouse-based aiming and skill activation. Use the movement controls to position Sil around enemy attacks, and use the dash to create distance or close gaps. The movement speed and unlimited dash options are most useful while learning boss patterns or navigating crowded encounters.

### Tools & Inventory

The main build system is managed through Sil's Armory and run rewards rather than a conventional item inventory. Select scissors, spells, and special skills before or during progression, then improve them with traits, imprints, relics, and other rewards. Use resource-related options carefully when selecting upgrades, curses, or other run choices.

### World & Environment

The Garden is divided into distinct domains and chapter-based routes filled with randomized encounters and rewards. Progress through the five-chapter main story and the three-part epilogue arc, defeating domain enemies and bosses along the way. Permanent upgrades remain available between attempts, while much of each individual run's build is assembled from randomized choices.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on giant scissors, witchcraft, special skills, and precise positioning. Enemies and bosses use distinctive attack patterns, transformations, and area attacks, so movement and timing remain important even when trainer options are active. Combine weapon damage, spell effects, cooldown abilities, traits, imprints, relics, and status-focused synergies to create stronger builds.
